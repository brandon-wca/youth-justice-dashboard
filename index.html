About the data

Source. All figures are from the New York State Division of Criminal Justice Services (DCJS), shared with a youth justice coalition WCA belongs to. The tables cover every New York county through .

Who is counted. An Adolescent Offender (AO) is a 16- or 17-year-old charged with a felony, a category created by Raise the Age in October 2018. Counts are arrests, not young people: someone arrested twice is counted twice.

"Stayed in criminal court." Everywhere on this page, the share of cases kept in criminal court leaves out cases the district attorney declined to prosecute, so New York City (where declinations are recorded) can be compared with the rest of the state (where they almost never are).

Years. DCJS does not say whether its year is the year of arrest or of disposition. Charts use the year as given by DCJS; the latest year may still be incomplete. 2020 and 2021 were COVID-19 years.

Small numbers. Many counties have only a handful of cases a year, so county figures use added together, and counties with fewer than 30 cases in the base are marked "too few cases".

Shares, not rates. A group's share of arrests is not an arrest rate; that would need the youth population by county and race, which this dashboard doesn't yet have. Sentencing figures are not adjusted for charge or prior record.

Tables: CUR-0010 (case outcomes) and CUR-0011 (arrests by race) in WCA's data warehouse. County outlines: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary file, 2025. Data file built .
